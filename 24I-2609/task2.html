Key Finding & Solution

Which penguin species is the heaviest, and does it also have the longest flippers?

Gentoo is the heaviest species with an average body mass of 5,076.0 g. YES! Gentoo also possesses the longest average flipper length at 217.2 mm (highlighted in Vibrant Blue).
